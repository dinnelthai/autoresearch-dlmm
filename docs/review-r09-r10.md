# R09/R10 historical research replay
POST /api/terminal/research-replay accepts pool, snapshotId and the explicitly checked UX draft. It requires a snapshot saved with matching review and hashed evidence. The snapshot reader limits names and bytes before reading, verifies manifest and files, pool/mint/slot/raw hash, review structure and time at capture, original raw capture and each referenced evidence hash. It runs the existing analyze-state --plans bridge against archived bytes, not live state. No new strategies or synthetic observations. Results are stored under data/research-runs/<pool>/<runId> with manifest, request, frozen state/review and report; relevant source hashes recorded before and after run. The UI shows a separate historical comparison, never sets current report, wallet binding or reviewId. Snapshot and replay may be used for alternate preview pools if cfg.researchReviews maps their exact addresses to local reviewed artifacts under data. Primary real-time research and monitoring are unchanged. No automatic construction of accepted review evidence; missing/expired/invalid observations still block, and archive integrity is not semantic validation. Preserve referenced raw archives. Replay has not been executed with live evidence; no claims of realized PnL, calibration or execution readiness.
Run node --test research/live/research-snapshot.test.mjs research/live/research-replay.test.mjs and full offline verifier. In the terminal select a pool, check explicit research budget and risk parameters, save a matched snapshot, then replay it. Switch A-B-A and verify each result remains scoped to the original pool and no historical result becomes a current review.
